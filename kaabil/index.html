The broken system

The problem isn't that
students forget.
It's that there's no proof.

Everything else — bad hiring, ghosting, fake resumes, GPA filtering — is just a consequence of one broken truth: knowledge lives in your head, invisible to everyone who needs to evaluate it.

📄
Resumes are claims
Anyone can write anything. There's no mechanism to verify the gap between what you say you know and what you actually know.
🎓
GPA measures compliance
A 9.2 CGPA student can fail a basic DSA interview. A 6.8 CGPA student can outcode them. The filter system never sees this.
🎲
Hiring is guesswork
Recruiters filter hundreds of resumes blindly. The best candidates get lost. The wrong ones get hired. Everyone loses.
💡
The root cause — and the fix
The system needs a new layer between education and employment. Not another portfolio tool. Not another job board. A verified, living, continuously updated proof of what you actually know. That's what KAABIL is.
How KAABIL works

Learning happens.
KAABIL makes it visible.

01
You finish an exam
KAABIL triggers a review session. No setup. No friction. The system runs inside your student lifecycle automatically.
02
AI acts as examiner
Not a tutor. Not a quiz. A calm, intelligent examiner that asks you to explain concepts in your own words — then probes deeper based on exactly what you said.
03
Your KAABIL Score updates
Five dimensions measured: Conceptual depth, Problem solving, Aptitude, Consistency, and Growth velocity. The score is longitudinal — it tells a story over time.
04
Recruiters discover you
Your public profile is searchable by verified skill. Recruiters don't post and pray. They search, filter by score, and contact only high-fit candidates.
05
Score decays if inactive
Skills aren't permanent. Your score reflects currency of knowledge — creating a natural pull to maintain it because it represents your career value.
